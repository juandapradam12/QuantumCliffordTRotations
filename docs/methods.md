# Definitions

Gates are matrices in the computational basis. A global phase is not a physical
difference: $U$ and $e^{i\alpha}U$ implement the same transformation of every
Bloch vector. Comparisons are invariant under that phase.

## Rotations

$$
R_x(\theta)=\begin{pmatrix}\cos(\theta/2)&-i\sin(\theta/2)\\-i\sin(\theta/2)&\cos(\theta/2)\end{pmatrix},\quad
R_y(\theta)=\begin{pmatrix}\cos(\theta/2)&-\sin(\theta/2)\\\sin(\theta/2)&\cos(\theta/2)\end{pmatrix},\quad
R_z(\theta)=\begin{pmatrix}e^{-i\theta/2}&0\\0&e^{i\theta/2}\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The Euler form of a general single-qubit unitary is

$$
U_3(\theta,\varphi,\lambda)=\begin{pmatrix}\cos(\theta/2)&-e^{i\lambda}\sin(\theta/2)\\e^{i\varphi}\sin(\theta/2)&e^{i(\varphi+\lambda)}\cos(\theta/2)\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Applied to $|0\rangle$,

$$
U_3(\theta,\varphi,\lambda)|0\rangle=\cos(\theta/2)|0\rangle+e^{i\varphi}\sin(\theta/2)|1\rangle,
$$

which does not depend on $\lambda$. The third angle shows up for any state with
support on both basis vectors.

The generators of the synthesis group are

$$
H=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix},\quad
S=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&i\end{pmatrix},\quad
T=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&e^{i\pi/4}\end{pmatrix}.
$$

They satisfy $S=T^2$, $Z=S^2$, $H=U_3(\pi/2,0,\pi)$, $S=U_3(0,0,\pi/2)$,
$T=U_3(0,0,\pi/4)$, and

$$
T=e^{i\pi/8}R_z(\pi/4),\qquad H R_z(\theta) H=R_x(\theta).
$$

The second identity is an equality of matrices. Together with the first,
$H T^k H$ is $R_x(k\pi/4)$ up to the phase $e^{ik\pi/8}$.

## Distance

The process fidelity of two single-qubit unitary channels is

$$
F(U,V)=\frac{1}{4}\left|\mathrm{Tr}(U^{\dagger} V)\right|^{2}.
$$

$F=1$ if and only if $V=e^{i\alpha}U$. Removing that phase, the relative gate
is a Bloch rotation by an angle $\delta\in[0,\pi]$,

$$
F=\cos^2(\delta/2),\qquad \delta=2\arccos\sqrt{F}.
$$

$\delta$ is the angle by which the approximation mis-rotates every Bloch vector.

## The set that is searched

The single-qubit Clifford group is the group generated by $H$ and $S$. It has
24 elements. Every Clifford+$T$ gate has a representative

$$
C_0\, T\, C_1\, T\, \cdots\, T\, C_t
$$

with each $C_i$ Clifford and with $t$ the minimal number of $T$ gates. $T^\dagger$
is included without a separate generator, because $T^\dagger=S^3 T$ and $S^3$ is
Clifford. $S=T^2$ is Clifford as well, so it is not charged as two $T$ gates.

Gates are identified by the rotation they induce on the Bloch sphere,
$R_{ba}=\frac{1}{2}\mathrm{Tr}(\sigma_b U\sigma_a U^{\dagger})$, which is blind
to global phase. The enumeration is breadth-first in $t$, so the recorded
$T$-count is minimal. Each reported word is one such factorization; its
Clifford factors are shortest words in $H$ and $S$. Up to the budgets used in
the figures the search is exhaustive: a missing gate would be a missing product
of this form, and every product has this form.
